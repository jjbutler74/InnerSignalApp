# Privacy Policy for InnerSignal

**Last updated: August 8, 2026**

InnerSignal ("the app") is a personal affirmation and gratitude journaling app. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

## Summary

InnerSignal does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data with its own servers or third parties. Your data lives on your device and may be included in Android's standard encrypted cloud backup, which you control.

## What data the app stores

InnerSignal stores the following information in a local database (SQLite) on your device. InnerSignal has no server and sends nothing to its own infrastructure. Your data may be included in Android's standard encrypted cloud backup (Google Drive), which preserves it when you switch phones or reinstall the app. You control this in your device's **Settings → Google → Backup**. If you turn Android backup off, your InnerSignal data stays strictly on-device.

- Your name, if you choose to enter one
- Your notification schedule preferences (affirmation times, gratitude time, quiet hours)
- Affirmation text, favorites, and how often you've seen each one
- Gratitude journal entries and mood selections
- Completion history used to calculate your streak

InnerSignal has no backend server, no user accounts, and no sign-in of any kind.

## Third-party services

InnerSignal does not use any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. No data is shared with third parties. The only external data flow is Android's own encrypted cloud backup, which is controlled by you in your device settings and is not accessible to InnerSignal or any third party.

## Permissions

InnerSignal requests a small number of Android permissions, used only as follows:

- **Notifications** — to deliver your scheduled affirmation and gratitude reminders.
- **Vibrate** — for notification feedback.
- **Receive boot completed** — to reschedule your reminders after your device restarts.

The app does not use these permissions to collect or transmit any personal information.

## Data deletion

You can erase all of your data at any time from **Settings → Start fresh**, which permanently deletes your journal entries, streak history, and preferences from the device. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data.

## Children's privacy

InnerSignal is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date.
